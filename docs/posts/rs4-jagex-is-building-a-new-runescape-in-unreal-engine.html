RS4: Jagex Is Building a New RuneScape in Unreal Engine

This post may include affiliate links. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Jagex just dropped a bombshell for one of gaming's most enduring communities: a fourth RuneScape title is officially in development, built on Unreal Engine. The studio is calling it RS4 for now, and while details are scarce, the announcement alone is enough to set the fanbase talking.

What We Know About RS4

Per Engadget, Jagex confirmed the project is underway and has chosen Unreal Engine as its foundation — a significant technical signal. RuneScape's history is defined by its browser-era origins and Java roots, later migrated through multiple engine overhauls. Choosing Unreal Engine from the ground up suggests RS4 is designed for a different visual and technical bar entirely.

Jagex says more details will come in 2025, so for now, RS4 is a name and a promise. But that promise carries weight given the franchise's track record of keeping millions of players engaged across two live games simultaneously.

Why Unreal Engine Matters Here

The engine choice isn't just a technical footnote. Unreal Engine 5 gives developers access to tools like Nanite and Lumen — features that power photorealistic geometry and dynamic lighting. For a franchise that has historically leaned into accessibility over visual fidelity, this signals a deliberate shift in ambition. RS4 is not going to look like the RuneScape you grew up with.

That's either exciting or alarming depending on who you ask. Long-time OSRS devotees have made clear they value the game's classic feel above all. But a new title — not a replacement — gives Jagex room to reach an audience that never bought into the older aesthetic.

Who Should Be Paying Attention

  • Existing RuneScape players — RS4 doesn't displace RS3 or Old School RuneScape. It's additive.
  • Lapsed fans — If the engine upgrade brings the visual polish of modern RPGs, this could be the re-entry point many drifted players have been waiting for.
  • The broader MMO market — A modernized RuneScape built on Unreal could genuinely compete for the attention of players currently cycling through survival games and live-service RPGs.

2025 is when Jagex says the curtain starts to lift. Until then, RS4 sits in that rare category: a confirmed game that's still almost entirely imagination. Given the franchise's staying power, that imagination is going to run wild.
